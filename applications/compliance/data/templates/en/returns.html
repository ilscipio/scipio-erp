Shipping and returns

Shipping

Shipping costs and delivery times are shown on the product page and in the checkout before you order.

Returns

You can return goods within {{returns.days}} days of delivery. Start a return in your account under "Orders", or use "Withdraw from contract here" at the bottom of every page.

Consumers in the EU also have the statutory right of withdrawal of {{withdrawal.days}} days; see the withdrawal policy. A voluntary return period does not limit it.

Packaging

Please reuse the shipping box for your return where you can. Dispose of packaging in the correct recycling stream; the product page names the materials.
