How we check sellers

{{store.name}} is a marketplace. Independent sellers offer some products. The product page always names the seller and says whether the seller is a business or a private person. EU consumer law, including the right of withdrawal, applies only to purchases from business sellers.

Before a seller can sell

  • We collect the seller's legal name, address, e-mail and phone number, a copy of an identity document for a person, the trade register and VAT numbers for a business, and the payment account.
  • We check this data against reliable sources and ask for corrections.
  • The seller confirms that it offers only products that comply with EU and US law.

What you can see

The seller page shows the legal name, address and contact of each business seller. High-volume sellers in the US must also show this data or allow us to show it.

Report a seller or a product

Write to {{store.email}} or use "Report this seller" on the seller page. We review every report and tell you what we decided.
